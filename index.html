Our editorial approach

Useful news starts
with clear context.

Our aim is to help readers discover reporting, understand where it comes from, and visit the original publisher for the complete article.

01 / ATTRIBUTION

Give credit clearly.

Publisher names and source links should help readers identify the organisation behind a story and reach the original article.

02 / CURRENCY

Keep news current.

We aim to make recent reporting easier to discover. Update timing and availability can depend on the publisher and source feed.

03 / TRANSPARENCY

Be clear about our role.

WYRIX News is a news discovery and aggregation service. Third-party reporting remains the work of its respective publishers.
