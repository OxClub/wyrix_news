Privacy Policy for Wyrix News

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Wyrix News ("we", "our", or "the app") is an Android news application with package name com.wyrix.news. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use our app.

1. News Sources and APIs

Wyrix News provides access to news content from more than 50 news websites and sources through APIs. These services help us retrieve and display available headlines, articles, summaries, images, and related information. The information available depends on the source and API provider.

API providers and news publishers may process technical information, such as IP addresses or request metadata, when their services are accessed. When you open a publisher's website or an external article, that publisher's privacy policy and practices apply.

We use API credentials where required to access news services. We do not intentionally publish private API credentials in this policy. The information collected and retained by each provider depends on that provider's own terms and privacy policy.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on app functionality and integrated services, information may include device or app information, IP address, usage events, crash reports, diagnostic information, and information required to retrieve news content or display advertisements. The exact information depends on the features used and the services integrated into the app.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

Wyrix News uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its partners may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising-related data to provide, measure, and improve advertising, subject to applicable settings and policies.

Learn more: Google Advertising Privacy and Terms and Google Privacy Policy .

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Retrieve and display news content through APIs.
  • Operate and maintain app features.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, and improve performance.

5. Sharing of Information

Information may be processed by API providers, advertising providers, news publishers, and other service providers when needed to provide app functionality. Their own privacy policies and terms may apply. We do not sell personal information as a standalone business practice.

6. Data Retention and Security

Retention periods depend on the type of information and the service processing it. We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Wyrix News is not specifically directed at children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information in a manner requiring action, please contact us.

8. Your Choices

Depending on your device, location, and applicable law, you may have options to control advertising personalization, app permissions, and certain data uses through your device settings or service-provider controls.

9. Third-Party Websites and Services

The app may link to third-party news websites and use external APIs. We do not control the privacy practices of those independent services. Please review their privacy policies when using them.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
